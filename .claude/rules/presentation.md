# Presentation rules

- No business rules and no SQL here. A widget shows state and sends events; a BLoC calls use cases. Never import Drift, `core/database` or a feature's `data/`.
- Every user-facing string comes from `context.l10n`. Add it to `app_en.arb` and `app_te.arb` in the same change. Telugu is a draft until reviewed; keep the wording plain and avoid accounting jargon.
- Use design-system pieces before writing new ones: `AppPrimaryButton`, `AppSecondaryButton`, `AppTextField`, `MoneyAmountField`, `MoneyText`, `BalanceSummary`, `EmptyState`, `ErrorView`, `LoadingView`, `InlineError`, `showConfirmDialog`, `DiscardChangesGuard`. Spacing, radius and sizes come from `AppSpacing`, `AppRadius`, `AppSizes`; colours from the theme and `context.semanticColors`. No literal colours or magic numbers.
- Every amount is shown with its direction in words (`BalanceSummary` / `l10n.balanceLabel`). Never colour or a minus sign alone. An advance is never labelled as an amount owed.
- Every screen handles loading, empty, failure and success. Failure text comes from `l10n.failureMessage(failure)`; never show exception text.
- BLoC state is immutable (`Equatable` or sealed classes). Form state keeps the field values, per-field errors and a submission status, so a failed save keeps what the owner typed.
- Submit events use `droppable()` from `bloc_concurrency`; the Save button uses `isLoading`. Generate the `operation_id` once when the form opens, not on each tap.
- Emit success only after the use case returns `Ok`.
- A form loads its data once with a repository `Future` (`getParty`), never `.first` of a watch stream. A screen that stays open subscribes to the stream and reads errors with `FailureException.failureOf`.
- Use `LoadStatus` and `SubmissionStatus` (`core/domain/submission_status.dart`) for screen and form state.
- Create a BLoC with `BlocProvider` at the route that needs it. Cancel stream subscriptions in `close()`. No global app BLoC for financial flows.
- Financial forms have a Review section (current balance, change, resulting balance) and are wrapped in `DiscardChangesGuard`. Never auto-save a financial entry.
- A row that shows an amount next to text must survive a huge amount at 2x text: give the amount side a `Flexible` + `FittedBox(scaleDown)` and put long text on its own full-width line. Never leave a text column unconstrained inside a `Row`.
- Show the result of a save as a snackbar on the page the owner returns to, with the new balance in words (`l10n.balanceText`). Snackbars are cleared automatically when a full page opens (`AppMessenger`).
- An entry form that can open without a party uses the party picker route (`AppRoutes.partyPick`), which also creates a party inline.
- A bottom sheet with fields and buttons is one scrolling list; a fixed footer does not fit at 2x text on a small phone.
- A generated string getter and an extension method on `AppLocalizations` must not share a name (the getter wins and the call fails to compile).
- A form that stays on screen after it has saved (for example under a follow-up dialog) must stop its Save spinner and disable Save; only forms that pop at once may treat `success` as busy.
- A dialog that saves keeps itself open and shows the failure until the save succeeds (see `_CategoryDialog`).
- Money-account balances can be negative: show them with `l10n.accountBalance`, not with a party-balance label.
- One primary action per screen. Confirmation dialogs name the action on both buttons; no bare "OK".
- Touch targets at least 48dp. Icon-only buttons have a `tooltip`. Layouts must survive Telugu at 2x text on a 320dp-wide screen: wrap text, avoid fixed heights, use `Flexible`/`Expanded`.
- A report screen states its period or "as of" date, that amounts are in rupees, and whether it shows balances or totals (`ReportNote`). Use `PeriodFilter` for a period; do not build another date-range control.
- Sharing goes through a cubit that mixes in `ShareActions`; a widget never calls `ShareService` or the plugin. Show no "sent" or "shared" message, only a failure to open the sheet. Build export text and CSV in `ReportExports` from the state on screen.
- A label that also appears in the Quick Add sheet needs its own shorter key on Home; two widgets with the same text make taps and tests ambiguous.
- A field for a PIN or passphrase uses `AppTextField(obscureText: true)`. Never put a PIN or passphrase in cubit state, a route, a log or a snackbar; pass it straight to the use case and drop it.
- Anything drawn above the navigator (the lock screen) needs its own `Overlay`, or its text fields have nowhere to put the cursor.
- Copy about backup, lock and reminders must not overclaim: say "encrypted with your passphrase", "covers the screen", "share sheet opened". Never "secure", "sent", "delivered" or that the records on the phone are encrypted.
- Navigate with `AppRoutes` constants. Routes are declared only in `lib/app/router/app_router.dart`.
- Never use the words Profit, Net worth, GST-compliant, Delivered, Synced or Cloud backup in V1 copy.
- Widget tests: English and Telugu, text scale 1 and 2, and the failure state.
